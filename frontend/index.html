Excel → CRM → Invoicing, without the copy-paste.

SyncLine is a working prototype that shows exactly how your customer data can flow automatically between a spreadsheet, your CRM, and your invoicing tool — no manual re-entry, no duplicate contacts, no missed invoices.

Scroll down to start syncing

Spreadsheet source

Start from a plain CSV or Excel export. New or updated rows are the trigger — no special formatting required beyond a few standard columns.

CRM sync

Every row becomes a contact. Matching by email means the same customer is updated, never duplicated, no matter how many times they reorder.

Invoicing

Deals with a value greater than zero automatically generate an invoice, ready to send — with the customer, product, and due date already filled in.

How it works
Three steps, fully automatic
1

Load your sheet

Paste your data or upload a CSV with customer name, email, product, and amount.

2

Run the sync

Each row is checked against existing contacts, deduplicated by email, and passed downstream.

3

Watch it flow

Contacts land in the CRM and qualifying deals generate invoices automatically — all logged in real time.
